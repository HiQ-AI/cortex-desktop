## v0.0.29 — 蜗牛有技能树了 🌳

蜗牛以前技能全开，脑子里七八种本事嗡嗡转。你只想查个碳足迹，它还惦记着做 PPT。

现在侧边栏多了个 **Skills**。点进去，技能卡片一排排摆好，不需要的关掉。下次对话蜗牛就不会分心了。

唯一关不掉的是 LCA 搜索——蜗牛的看家本领，关了等于裁员。

### 🌳 技能中心
- 卡片式布局，开关一拨，新对话生效
- 7 个内置技能：LCA 搜索（Core）、Excel、PDF、Word、PPT、前端设计、产品知识
- 全局统一管理，不再每个目录拷贝一份

蜗牛有技能树了。目前只有一层，还不能加点。🐌

### 平台
- **macOS** — arm64 | **Windows** — x64 | **Linux** — x64

---

The snail used to have all skills always on — seven abilities buzzing while you just wanted a carbon footprint lookup.

Now there's a **Skills** button in the sidebar. Cards in a grid, toggles on each. Turn off what you don't need. Next conversation, the snail focuses.

Only skill you can't turn off: LCA Search. Core competency. Disabling it would be a layoff.

### 🌳 Skills Center
- Card grid with toggles, takes effect in new conversations
- 7 built-in skills: LCA Search (Core), Excel, PDF, Word, PPT, Frontend Design, Product Knowledge
- Single source of truth — no more per-workspace copies

Skill tree unlocked. One tier. No skill points yet. 🐌
